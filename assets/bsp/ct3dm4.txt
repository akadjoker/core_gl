play information:

tourney:          1on1
deathmatch:       Small DM (up to 6 ppl)
team deathmatch:  3vs3
clan arena:	  3vs3
CTF:              no
Bot File (aas):   yes
other:            Be sure to visit the secret room :)
new sounds:       no
new graphics:     yes
new music:        no

how to play:      place ct3dm4.pk3 in your /baseq3/ folder
                  start quake3/Live
                  hit ~
                  type \map ct3dm4
                  type \addbot <botname> <skill>

                  hit ~ (to clear the console)
                 
                  OR select Paranoid(ct3dm4) from the skrimish menu
